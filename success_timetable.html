⚑ Timetable Rules & Notes
  • Meals are fixed at ~11AM, ~3PM, ~9PM but may shift ±30 min if a session runs long — never skip.
  • S_Techy runs Mon–Fri at 11:30 AM without exception. It is the daily anchor.
  • Wednesday and Saturday are Coding Days. Protect them fiercely — no social bookings during coding blocks.
  • HAI Arch (4–6h) can expand to 6h on days with more room — Mon and Sun are ideal for this.
  • Occupied timeslots are absolute locks. Nothing gets scheduled there, ever.
  • Duolingo and Book Review are dailies — if they miss their assigned slot, they must be done before 1AM.
  • Light Nap is scheduled Tue/Thu + 2 more days of your choice — never skip all 4 in a week.
  • Free / Flex blocks are NOT optional tasks time — they are buffer zones. Use them for overflow only if needed.
  • Certs runs 4x/week. Mon, Tue, Fri are fixed; the 4th session can float to any remaining day without a lock conflict.
  • People Check-Ups (3h, 2x/week) is large — Friday holds one session; the 2nd can shift to Mon or Sat Social window.
  • Veto Academy runs Tue, Thu, Sun — 1.5h each, no breaks. These are content-creation discipline sessions.
  • Thrivers Trybe is locked to Friday at 6:30 PM. It is the weekly social anchor — non-negotiable.
  • If a Coding Day falls behind, do NOT extend past 1AM. Carry debt into next session, not into sleep.
  • Sunday 11PM–1AM is Week Prep time. Use it to review, adjust, and plan the week ahead.
